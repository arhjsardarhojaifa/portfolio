My Story

The journey has only just begun.

I want to see the world beyond the familiar—one destination, one experience and one story at a time.

01 • Who I Am

Curious about what makes every place different.

ARHJ Sardar Hojaifa is a travel explorer, filmmaker and storyteller from Bangladesh, driven by a curiosity to discover the diversity of the world. He is interested in places, history, heritage, culture, traditions, nature and the stories of the people who make each destination unique.

Through travel, photography and filmmaking, he wants to document his journeys and share the world from his perspective.

02 • Why I Travel

To see the world beyond the familiar.

I travel because I want to see the world beyond the familiar.

I want to walk through places I have only read about, experience different cultures and traditions, discover forgotten history, spend time in nature and understand how people live in different parts of the world.

Travel also changes the way I see ordinary things. It teaches me to be curious, observant and open to perspectives different from my own.

My goal is not simply to collect destinations. I want to collect experiences, stories and a deeper understanding of the diverse world we live in.

03 • My Story

One destination, one experience, one story at a time.

My journey with travel began with a simple curiosity about the world beyond what I could see around me. I have always been fascinated by the fact that every place has its own history, culture, traditions, landscapes and stories.

For me, travelling is not simply about visiting famous destinations or taking photographs. It is about experiencing a place, observing its people and understanding what makes it different from anywhere else. A historical building can tell a story of centuries, a village can reveal a different way of life, and even an ordinary journey can become a memorable experience.

I am still at the beginning of my journey. There are countless places I have yet to see and stories I have yet to discover. This website is a place to document that journey—one destination, one experience and one story at a time.

04 • My Vision

A long road ahead.

My journey has only just begun.

I want to gradually explore Bangladesh, then travel beyond its borders and experience different parts of Asia, Europe, Africa, the Americas and the rest of the world. I want to visit places that carry history, experience cultures that are different from my own, and discover landscapes that I have never seen before.

Along the way, I want to develop myself as a filmmaker, photographer and storyteller, documenting the places and experiences I encounter. My long-term vision is to build a global travel brand that tells meaningful stories about our diverse world through authentic experiences and creative storytelling.

I do not know exactly where the journey will take me. That is part of what makes it worth beginning.
